How to Make Gelatine Bioplastic

What Is Gelatine Bioplastic? 🍮

Gelatine bioplastic is a biodegradable material made from gelatine, water, and a plasticiser such as glycerine. When heated with water, the gelatine dissolves into a liquid mixture. As it cools and dries, it forms a thin solid sheet. Glycerine helps make the material softer and more flexible.

Materials 🥄

  • 10g gelatine powder
  • 60ml water
  • 4–5ml glycerine
  • A heat-safe container
  • A stirring tool
  • A flat, smooth surface or mold

Instructions 🧪

  1. Mix 10g of gelatine powder, 60ml of water, and 4–5ml of glycerine in a heat-safe container.

  2. Let the gelatine absorb the water for 2–3 minutes.

  3. Heat the mixture gently in a microwave or hot water bath until the gelatine is fully dissolved.

  4. Stir the mixture until it is smooth and lump-free.


  1. Pour the liquid onto a flat, smooth surface.

  2. Spread the mixture evenly into a thin layer.

  3. Leave the bioplastic to dry for 24–48 hours.

  4. Once completely dry, carefully peel the finished sheet away from the surface.

Tips and Warnings 💡

  • Heat the mixture gently.
  • Stir until there are no visible lumps.
  • Spread the liquid as evenly as possible.
  • Thicker areas may take longer to dry.
  • Thin edges may dry faster and curl upward.
  • Wait until the material is completely dry before peeling it off.

Results ✨

Original Version

This is the original version without added color. The material becomes more translucent as it dries.


Colored Version

Food coloring can also be added to the mixture to create different colored versions of the bioplastic.
