A Little History of MoMA

The Museum of Modern Art (MoMA) was founded in New York City in 1929. It was created with the idea of establishing a museum dedicated to modern art and has since become one of the most influential modern and contemporary art museums in the world. Its collection includes painting, sculpture, photography, film, design, architecture, and many other forms of visual culture.
