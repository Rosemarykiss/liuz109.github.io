The second floor features works from the 1980s to the present, introducing us to contemporary art through painting, sculpture, photography, installation, and other media. This is where our tour of MoMA's collection begins!



2nd Floor (Entire Floor)



Works from the Collection 1980–Today


Arranged in loosely chronological order, each of the galleries on this floor explores an individual topic from the contemporary moment. A room may be devoted to a single artist or work of art, a particular place or a moment in time, a specific medium, or a shared creative idea.

These interdisciplinary presentations are conceived by curators from across the Museum who collaborate to share insights and perspectives. A program of frequent reinstallation brings together artworks from around the world in new combinations, presenting a dynamic array of ideas and histories.
